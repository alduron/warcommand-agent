Keyboard commands. The menu is connected.

The menu, its tree and every outcome it can produce were written and tested and
nothing ever constructed them: the composition root passed null where the menu
belongs, so no digit reached anything and there was no way to make a request,
take one, or close one without the web board. That is the half that was
missing, and this is it.

TAP push-to-talk. It latches the menu open and stays open with the key
released, so the whole thing is a keyboard surface and needs no microphone.
Holding it is still the voice path: hold, speak, let go on the confirm.

Make a request: tap, pick the category, pick the type, type the grid, and it
sends on confirm. The coordinate is taken the moment the key goes down and is
never resampled while you read the menu.

Take one or close one: tap, then 0 for the board, then the slot digit, then
1 ACCEPT, 2 START, 3 DONE, 4 PASS, 5 RELEASE, 6 MUTE, 7 COPY.

Backspace goes up a level, Escape closes, and a menu left open closes itself.

Bind push-to-talk to a keyboard key if you do not want a mouse button: tray,
Settings, KEYBINDS, click the chord, press the key. Any key works, and nothing
about the menu needs a microphone.
