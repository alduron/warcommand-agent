Keybinds you can actually set, and a header you can read.

The keybinds tab could show a chord and never set one, and nothing was saved
between launches, so push-to-talk stayed unbound and no key did anything on
any machine. Click a chord to rebind it, press the key or mouse button, and it
is kept. Esc cancels, and a chord another binding holds is refused by name.

Hotkeys work without the game. Every binding except Panic is inert unless
Wardogs is the foreground window, and Wardogs is not out. While the overlay is
Always on, which is you saying you run it without the game, the gate answers
yes. Mirroring keeps the strict rule.

The header hint is no longer clipped. Fourteen subscribed roles ate the row
and cut the line that says why the keyboard does nothing. The roles wrap now
and the hint sizes first.

The invite code is the largest thing on the top bar and the word "invite" is
gone. It is the one string on the surface you read out loud.

Changing a role on the website updates the overlay. The header kept its old
glyphs because subscribed roles only ever arrive on the config read, and a
role change never triggered one.

The countdown glides instead of stepping once a second.

Panic, since it is on the tray: one press, RightAlt+P, suspends every hook,
capture, draw and audio path at once. It is the only binding that stays live
when the game is not in front of you.
